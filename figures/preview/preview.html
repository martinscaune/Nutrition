Food value preview

A side exploration of about 40 foods. Hover any point for its details. Not project results.

Caveats: prices are placeholder estimates of Latvian prices, not observations. Some protein digestibility values are proxies. "Useful protein" = protein × min(DIAAS,100)/100 for the food eaten alone; in a mixed diet, complementary proteins do better. Calories and grams per 100 g are for the food as eaten (cooked rice, not dry). Per-euro values don't depend on cooking, since water adds neither calories nor cost.
